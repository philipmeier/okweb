Character Consistency

AI Characters That Stay Consistent Shot to Shot

When you prompt Kling or Runway directly, your character's face shifts between takes. 10zebra's image-first reference pipeline locks it.

  • ✓ Reference portraits — generate a character image once, then anchor every subsequent shot to it automatically.
  • ✓ Character Swap — drop in a reference photo to replace the person in any video while preserving all original motion.
  • ✓ Environment locking — maintain lighting, color palette, and art direction across your whole storyboard.
  • ✓ Multi-reference editing — blend up to 3 references (character, costume, environment) into a single new frame.

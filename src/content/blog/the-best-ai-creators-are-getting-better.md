### tool capacity (x) creator strategy

There are two main number of reasons why we will keep seeing improvements.

The tools are getting better. They will increase resolution, quality, coherence, duration, control, usability, etc.

(x)

Creators are optimizing their own creative strategies for managing new workflows. I don't just mean there are more creators rolling dice. There is growing expertise in managing the new pipelines.

This later, artist driven creative process preserves the soul of the content. There is a person in the drivers seat pushing towards a vision, coupled to the generative flow of whats possible and what works together.

### Just getting started

Why do we know the tools will get better? It's almost manifest desitiny. It sits on a curve that scales with information science. Its scaling along with other AI tools chains, which already have huge market forces. Let's lists them out:

-Compute is getting better

-Data is curated better

-Training costs are dropping

-Inference is getting faster, and will be more edge efficient

-The number of brilliant minds innovating in this field is increasing

-R&D budgets across all incumbents betting on the future

-Open source is pushing innovation (This is amazing, and good for mitigating tech monopolies)

-Even brand prestige and retention play a roll in turning the whee faster (e.g. the best engineers are more enticed to work at tech companies that publish openly)

It's not going to slow down. So grab your surf board, and ride this creative wave with us!

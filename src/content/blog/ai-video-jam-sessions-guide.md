## What are AI Video Jam Sessions?

AI Video Jam sessions bring together artists, tech enthusiasts, and creators to generate AI-powered video content collaboratively.

Hosted on Discord, Participants break out into teams to create unique AI video projects within a set time frame—30 seconds in 30 minutes.

AI Video Jams are the best place for knowledge-sharing on technology’s frontline. These sessions provide an excellent opportunity to learn from community members and share what you know.

### 🏁 AI Video Jam Session General Rules

#### Teams

****Participants are assigned to teams. If you have a team, great. If you are solo, we love that and we will assign you to a team.

#### Duration

****The total event is 1 hour, and the breakout competition is 30 minutes.

#### Objective

****Create a 20-30 second video based on a provided audio clip. Everyone gets the same audio. There are different audio tracks and themes each week.

#### Tools

****Use 10zebra’s AI storyboard interface for video creation and collaboration. Use any other tools you want, but the final project must be exported from 10zebra. some text

- We also recommend free video stock sites like [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/videos/), [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/videos/), and [Videezy](https://www.videezy.com/).
- Use the Midjourney Channel in 10zebra’s Discord server, or any other image generator, and upload directly into 10zebra.
- Take videos directly into the 10zebra app by logging into [10zebra](http://www.10zebra.app) on your mobile device and select the camera icon  📷 Keep the video short, under 5 seconds!

#### Submission

****Team leads are responsible for submitting your final video by the end of the session. The current week’s submissions will be viewed and voted on at the beginning of the following week’s AI Video Jam Session.

#### Weekly Winners

****Weekly winners are automatically entered into 10zebra's Quarterly AI Video Showcase, where guests can watch and upvote videos over the week of the Showcase, culminating in a Discord party where winners are announced and prizes awarded, including **10zebra AI Artist Sponsorship** packages.

## 🔎 How to Join and Navigate AI Video Jam Sessions

You must sign up for and Log in to both **10zebra** and **Discord**

- **Create a 10zebra account:**[10zebra.app](http://10zebra.app)

- **Join 10zebra's Server:** [Click here to join our server](https://discord.gg/7PCt4Xqw7k).

#### 🧭 Navigating Discord

- **👯**[**Join 10zebra Discord Server**](https://discord.gg/7PCt4Xqw7k)
- Navigate to the **Event Page** on the 10zebra Discord server, and click on the event **Invite Link** shared on the page.

Use **Text Channels**

- To 😜 **chat** with team members and share media files and contacts
- To view and  ✅ **vote**on AI Video Jam submissions from the previous week

Use **Voice Channels** to talk, watch videos, and collab in breakouts

- **Breakout Channels:** During the session, you will be directed to a breakout channel for team collaboration. They are found under Voice Channels
- **Returning to Main Event:** Use the channel list to navigate back to the main **AI Video Jam 10zebra Voice Channel** when needed.

#### 🦓 Quick Guide to the 10zebra Interface

Open the Project Link

For these events, you won't start a new project. You will be given a project link found on the **AI Video Jam Chat** under **Text Channels**.

- The team lead will own the project, and collaborators will have limited functionality.
- You won't upload the audio, as all that will already be done.
- The project will already be storyboarded with empty shots.
**Upload Content**

****Open a shot and upload a video.

</div>**How to Use the Different AI Models**

- **Text-to-Video:**Add a text prompt to transform your ideas into video clips through AI capabilities.
- **Video-to-Video:** Add a text prompt, a video reference, and set the influence level to transform your content through AI capabilities.
- **AutoCine:** Add a text prompt and a video reference. Autocine will base an entire clip on just the first frame with higher resolution.

This is a story built out of my own experience.   Every scene was filmed on a mobile phone during the conference and then immediately transformed into an AI animation, using 10zebra’s app.

I’d like to point out the symbolism to me, drawing from Renard Jenkins Keynote speech.  I am thankful that the conference was not dominated by the sense of doom and hype found in much of social media.  Rather, he spoke with a cautious optimism.

“Can I film you pretending you are ‘rushing a script?”

That’s how I met new friends while waiting in line for coffee at “AI on the Lot,”  and how this AI powered music video together during yesterdays all day event between AI & Hollywood.

Industry vets from studios …  ai creators known by their discord handle … yesterdays all day event brought together AI & Hollywood.

In Renard Jenkins’ keynote address, he made the point that the idea “Hollywood is Over” is false, just like the idea that AI is a person. But how we create media is surely changing.

“It’s just math” he said in front of a wall of equations that are not even equations. He was illustrating the point that AI models are statistical machines, tools in our control.

Looking down at my own cell phone, I felt empowered by the technology.   Using 10zebra for creative expression - GPUs were flipping bits thousands of miles away, and then new visuals would arrive on my phone, updating the realtime music video.

The best things about this community:  everyone’s curiosity and openness to sharing knowledge.  It might be starting with a silly idea. ( e.g. “AI Nerds meet the Apocalypse”) .. but everyone is helping each other level up.  Using constraints as an additional creative accelerant.

Personally, I’m working to make a new music video every week, and I’m all about showing you how you can do it too.

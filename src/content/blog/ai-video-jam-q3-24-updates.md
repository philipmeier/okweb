## AI Video Jam Weekly Highlights and Winners

#### Each week, we will showcase notable submissions from participants. These creative videos demonstrate the diverse talents and innovative approaches within our community. 🏆 August 8th: "Salt Mine" by FCKYES

Our August 8th AI Video Jam was a thrilling competition where Team A and Team B went head-to-head to create stunning visuals for the track "Salt Mine" by DJ duo FCKYES. Both teams had just 30 minutes to storyboard, create, and assemble their videos using 10zebra’s powerful AI tools.

Team A impressed with their dynamic visuals that perfectly matched the energetic beats of the track. Meanwhile, Team B wowed us with their creative use of AI-generated elements, bringing a goofy, trippy flair to their video.

This competition showed off the incredible potential of real-time collaboration and AI-assisted creativity within the 10zebra ecosystem.

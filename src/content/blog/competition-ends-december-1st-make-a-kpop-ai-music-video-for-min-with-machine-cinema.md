You can try a prompt with text-to-video. If its not right, you can tweak the prompt and try again. Often another take with the same prompt is sensible. For this project we recommend Flyper as a starting point.

<div>

![](/src/assets/blogs/competition-ends-december-1st-make-a-kpop-ai-music-video-for-min-with-machine-cinema-inline-2.png)

</div>The default text-to-image would generate a video like the one on the lower left. 

Let's unpack the screen above. The prompt is set to generate using Text to Video with "Flyper." This will interpret your prompt with text to image using Flux Pro. Then provide the image to Haiper's for image to video generation. If you would rather use Luma or Runway as your video engine, then switch to "Fluma" or "Funway."

If you are particularly fond of another image generator (Midjourney, anyone?), or created an asset by some other means (eyes on you, creative partners with early access), you can drag any image or video into "Add take." Images will turn into a very short ken burns video. Typically, the next step is then to animate that image to have better motion dynamics with one of the models shown below.

<div>

![](/src/assets/blogs/competition-ends-december-1st-make-a-kpop-ai-music-video-for-min-with-machine-cinema-inline-3.png)

</div>The selected model &quot;1st Frame Luma&quot; would take the first frame of the video (e.g an uploaded image), and animate it with novel motion dynamics from the prompt.

This is also a handy way to leverage a nice composition from a previous text to video generation, but to roll the dice for better motion dynamics. "Camera orbit right" is a great three-word prompt for Luma. "action movie" is a nice phrase to append after "woman breakdancing" on Runway or Haiper.

## Shimmy AI Music Video Competition

01. **Overview**

- Focus on highlighting themes of self-confidence, fun, and youthful energy.
- Adherence to modern hip-hop and K-pop fusion sounds.

#### 02. Target Audience

- **Genre:** Modern hip-hop, Fusion K-pop
- **Sound:** Energetic beat with a strong bass line
- **Vibe:** Tease, dance, shake your body, and bring a “do what I want” attitude

#### 03. Visual Style Details

- **Tone and Mood:** Fun, bright colors, playful, full of self-confidence.
- **Cultural Reference:** Incorporate the "Young-shimmy-shimmy-shimmy" theme inspired by a popular Korean patty-cake game featuring Young Shim, a fun-loving 13-year-old girl.
- **Avoid:** Sci-fi/fantastical elements; focus on realism and playful settings.

#### 04. Submission Requirements

- **Duration:** 30 seconds to 1 minute
- **Deadline:** Submit by December 1
- **Confidentiality:** Entries must not be shared publicly before the competition concludes.
- **Posting Rules:** Creators may post their videos before or after the competition, provided they give proper credit.

#### 05. Song Details

- **Title:** Shimmy
- **Album:** PRIME TIME
- **Artist:** Min (@therealminnn)
- **Producer:** MONSTAR Entertainment (@be.monstar)

#### 06. Judging Criteria

- **Originality:** Creative interpretation and unique execution.
- **Adherence to Genre:** Captures the spirit of modern hip-hop and K-pop fusion.
- **Visual Appeal:** Aesthetically engaging and cohesive.
- **Message Alignment:** Emphasizes themes of fun, confidence, and “do what I want” attitude.

#### 07. Awards and Recognition

- **Prize:** $1,000
- **Opportunity:** Possible collaboration with the artist (Min) on an official music video.This is optional and not guaranteed.
- Artist’s expectations will guide the final decision.

#### Additional Notes

- **Prohibited Content:** No specific restrictions on images, but content must align with themes and tone of the competition.

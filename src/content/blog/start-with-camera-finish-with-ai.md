The distinction between "production" and "post" is blurring.  I find **the iterative creative loop** can spin much faster with AI.  Speed drives cycles of evaluation and action; and that drives quality.   Well, as long as it's under creative control. That’s what 10zebra helps with.

I love the creative control of **framing relevant details with a camera** - finding light and color and texture and context in the real world.

See that dark nook?  We can turn it into a cave.  A few moments later,  when I am still in the forest, an AI horse rides into the reimagined cave.  **The forest itself becomes my style mood board**.  A small rock becomes a cliff. The world, the camera, the prompt, a suite of video models and my aesthetic judgement - are all part of a creative flow.

Here is the workflow I used in this demo:

- **Film a clip** to select relevant details.  I used my cell phone.
- **Edit Image** via prompt - to add characters, or change viewpoints
- **Image to Video** - from a suite of video models at your fingertips

I have worked hard to create a playground that I can share with you.  I invite you!  Film. Transform. Reimagine.  All from a website that works on your desktop and mobile.

As you can imagine, work is spit between having a good plan, determining core images and using those as references.

You can do all all your image generation, asset management and video generation with us. Even shared across teams.

When new models come out, you can keep your workflow and organization. Just switch the model.  It is often the case the clients who pay for work have an opinion about the model that is used. 10zebra lets you keep your workflow and just switch to the relevant video model.

Sound edits are best handled off-platform, in your post production. That is, we only handle 1 master soundtrack - enough for a music videos in our live collaboration. And while we let you create sound inside each generation (the multimodal models can do that). My personal advice: ask for sound effects and voice in your video generation, but no background music. Why? Sound effects become choppy across scenes and interfere with a subsequent edit. Whereas voice without background sound is more versatile..
